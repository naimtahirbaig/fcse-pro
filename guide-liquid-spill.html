Liquid spilled on a laptop

Act in the first minutes. Every minute the power stays on raises the damage.

5 minutes · Urgent

  1. Power the laptop off straight away. Hold the power button until it switches off.
  2. Unplug the charger and every other cable. Take out the battery if it is removable.
  3. Turn the laptop upside down in a tent shape, with the keyboard facing down, so the liquid drains out. Do not turn it back over yet.
  4. Wipe the outside with a dry cloth. Do not use a hair dryer or leave it on a heater.
  5. Do not switch it on to see if it works. Power on a wet board can cause short circuits.
  6. Bring it to us as soon as you can. Sweet drinks, tea, coffee and salt water leave residue that keeps damaging the board.

Bring it to us if

  • Always. Even if it seems to work, liquid can damage the board later. The sooner it is cleaned, the better the chance of saving it.
