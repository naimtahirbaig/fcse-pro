Cracked laptop screen

What to do straight after a crack, and what to bring to the shop.

10 minutes · Easy

  1. Stop pressing the screen and do not close the lid hard. Cracks spread when the laptop is flexed.
  2. If glass is chipping or loose, do not use the laptop, so you do not cut your fingers.
  3. Back up your files if the laptop still works.
  4. To find out if only the screen is broken, connect an external monitor or TV with an HDMI cable. If the picture appears there, the laptop itself is working.
  5. Write down the model number from the sticker on the bottom of the laptop. Screens are specific to each model.
  6. Bring the laptop to us for a quote on a replacement screen.

Bring it to us if

  • The screen shows lines, colour patches or black areas.
  • Nothing shows on the external monitor either, which suggests a different fault.
