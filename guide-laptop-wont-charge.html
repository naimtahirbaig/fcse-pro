Laptop not charging

Work through the simple checks before you buy a new charger or battery.

10 minutes · Easy

  1. Plug the charger into a different wall socket, or test the socket with another device. A dead socket or power strip is the most common cause.
  2. Look at the charger cable, the brick and the tip. If you see bends, cuts, burn marks or a loose tip, stop using it.
  3. Unplug the charger and remove USB devices. Hold the power button for 30 seconds, then plug the charger in again. If your laptop has a removable battery, take it out first and put it back after the reset.
  4. Check that the charger matches your laptop. The voltage and watt rating printed on the charger should match the label on your laptop. Do not use a charger with different voltage.
  5. Look into the charging port with a torch. Lint can stop the plug seating fully. Do not push metal objects into the port.
  6. Wiggle the plug gently while it is connected. If charging starts and stops, the cable or the port is loose.

Bring it to us if

  • The charger or the laptop gets very hot.
  • The battery looks swollen, or the case or trackpad is lifting. Stop using it and unplug it straight away.
  • The port feels loose or the plug does not stay in.
  • Nothing changes after a different charger of the correct rating.
