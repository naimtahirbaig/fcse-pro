Back up your files before a repair

Protect your photos and documents before any repair, upgrade or reinstall.

30 minutes · Easy

  1. Plug in an external hard disk or a large USB drive.
  2. Copy these folders: Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures and Videos.
  3. Check any other places you save work, such as the Music folder, WhatsApp backups or project folders on another drive.
  4. Export your browser bookmarks, and write down the names of programs you need to install again.
  5. Open a few of the copied files from the backup drive to check they work.
  6. Keep the backup somewhere safe and separate from the laptop.

Bring it to us if

  • The laptop will not start or the drive is not detected. Do not keep trying, bring it to us and ask for data recovery.
  • You hear clicking sounds from the drive.
