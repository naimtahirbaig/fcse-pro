Laptop overheating or loud fan

Heat and fan noise usually mean blocked vents or old thermal paste.

15 minutes · Easy to medium

  1. Use the laptop on a hard, flat surface. Beds and sofas block the air vents underneath.
  2. Check what is using the processor in Task Manager. Close any program using a lot of CPU when you are not using it.
  3. Look at the air vents. If they are clogged with dust, switch the laptop off, unplug it and take out the battery if you can. Use short bursts of compressed air. Hold the fan still with a cotton bud so it does not spin too fast.
  4. Do not open a sealed laptop if you are not sure how. Opening it can cause damage.
  5. Make sure the power plan is not set to maximum performance when you are only browsing.

Bring it to us if

  • The laptop shuts down on its own from heat.
  • The fan is loud all the time even when you are doing nothing.
  • It is more than two or three years old and has never been cleaned. A clean and fresh thermal paste often helps.
