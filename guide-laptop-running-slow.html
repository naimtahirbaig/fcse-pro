Laptop running slow

Free up space, stop what starts with Windows, and find out whether an upgrade will help.

20 minutes · Easy

  1. Restart the laptop. Many slowdowns are fixed by a clean start.
  2. Check free disk space in File Explorer. Try to keep at least 15 percent of the drive free by removing files you no longer need.
  3. Open Task Manager (Ctrl + Shift + Esc), go to the Startup tab, and turn off programs you do not need at start-up.
  4. Install pending Windows updates, then run a full scan in Windows Security.
  5. Close extra browser tabs and extensions. Many tabs can use several gigabytes of memory.
  6. Check what your laptop has inside. A laptop with a spinning hard disk usually becomes much faster with an SSD. If it has 4 GB of RAM, more memory will help too.

Bring it to us if

  • It is still slow after these steps.
  • It makes clicking or grinding noises, which can mean a failing hard disk. Back up your files first.
  • You see pop-ups or strange programs you did not install.
